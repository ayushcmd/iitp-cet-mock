Discover IIT Patna CET’s 9 Senate-approved academic degree programs, modular multi-exit learning pathways, and specialized technology curriculum.

B.Tech / B.S. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AIDS)

Undergraduate • 4 Years

A premier undergraduate engineering program integrating deep theoretical foundations of machine learning, deep learning, statistical modeling, big data pipelines, and intelligent cloud systems.

Undergraduate • 4 Years

Postgraduate • 2 Years
