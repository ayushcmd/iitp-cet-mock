Admission Notice

Official notifications, session deferrals, and admission circulars for hybrid programs at IIT Patna

Educational Replica Notice: This page is an informational archive replicating official admission notices issued by IIT Patna Centre for Educational Technology. Applications and fee transactions must only be conducted through the official registration portal (registrations.iitp-cep.in).

Important Notification Regarding New Admissions July 2025 (Autumn Session)

We at IIT Patna are thrilled to announce that all seats for the July 2025 (Autumn 2025) session are filled. As a result, all registrations that have not paid their admission fees or met the eligibility criteria are automatically deferred for the December/ January 26 (Spring 2026) Winter Session, effective from 9th August 2025.

Active Intake Cycle

Spring 2026 (Winter Intake) Registrations

Apply Now ↗

Following complete seat fulfillment of the Autumn 2025 cohort, applications are currently accepted for the upcoming Spring 2026 intake across all premier undergraduate and postgraduate programs:

  • B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS) — 4-year hybrid degree program.
  • B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) — 4-year hybrid degree program.
  • Executive M.Tech in AI & Data Science Engineering — 2-year hybrid program for working professionals.
  • Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) — 3-year hybrid undergraduate management program.

💡 Important Note: Applicants deferred from Autumn 2025 need not re-register. Your application ID remains valid for Spring 2026 seat allotment upon document verification.
