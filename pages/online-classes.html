❓ Moodle FAQs for Students

Quick answers to common questions related to Moodle

Moodle is an online Learning Management System (LMS) used to access course content, submit assignments, take quizzes, and interact with teachers.

Students receive login credentials on their official IIT Patna student email account during program enrollment. Access the official portal at cet.iitp.ac.in/moodle/ and enter your username and temporary password.

Use the "Forgotten your username or password?" link on the Moodle login screen to request a reset link to your registered webmail. If issues persist, email moodle_support_ug@iitp.ac.in with your Roll Number and full name.

Course enrollments are synchronized automatically within 48 to 72 hours of semester fee verification. If your course is not visible after this period, please contact your course coordinator or email support.

Navigate to your course page, locate the designated assignment link, click "Add submission", upload your required file (typically PDF format), and click "Save changes" before the specified submission deadline.

Open your course, find the scheduled quiz module, read instructions and time constraints carefully, click "Attempt quiz now", complete your answers, and ensure you click "Submit all and finish" prior to timer expiration.

Yes. You can access the LMS through any mobile browser, or install the official Moodle Mobile App (available on iOS and Android) and connect to server address: https://cet.iitp.ac.in/moodle/.

Common accepted formats include PDF documents (.pdf), text documents (.doc/.docx), and zip archives (.zip). Always refer to individual assignment instructions for specific restrictions and file size limits.
💡 Tip: Check the Moodle Calendar regularly and enable email/app notifications to stay updated on assignments, quizzes, and live announcements.
