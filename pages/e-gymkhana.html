Digital platform for student governance, clubs, societies, and extracurricular life at IIT Patna

Digital E-Gymkhana Under Preparation

The Centre for Educational Technology is developing a dedicated digital ecosystem to connect online and hybrid students with IIT Patna's vibrant student governance, cultural festivals, sports, and technical societies.

Students' Gymkhana at IIT Patna

The Students' Gymkhana at IIT Patna is the apex governing body responsible for fostering holistic student development across cultural, technical, sports, and social dimensions. When operational, the E-Gymkhana portal will provide digital infrastructure for:

💻

Technical Council

Workshops, national hackathons, coding contests, robotics competitions, and collaborative open-source projects hosted by technical clubs.

🎭

Cultural Council

Music, dramatics, visual arts, literature, debating, and annual campus celebrations including Anwesha, the premier socio-cultural festival.

🏆

Sports Council

Athletics, intramural leagues, yoga sessions, fitness challenges, and collegiate representation in the prestigious Inter-IIT Sports Meet.

🤝

Student Welfare

Peer-to-peer mentoring programs, academic counseling, mental well-being initiatives, and onboarding support for remote learners.

Stay Connected

Announcements regarding club inductions, elections, and digital activities will be published on the CET Notice Board and broadcasted via the official student email directory. For inquiries, reach out to the Academic Section, Admin Block 3rd Floor, IIT Patna.
