Bonafide Application

Procedures and guidelines for requesting an official institutional bonafide certificate

Read-Only Informational Mock: This page outlines the certificate request procedure and required student records. In compliance with student privacy principles, this replica does NOT collect, store, or transmit personal student information. To submit an official bonafide application, use the verified Microsoft Forms link below.

Bonafide Request Application

Students enrolled in programs at IIT Patna can apply for a bonafide certificate using the button below.

Instructions:

  1. Please ensure you fill all details correctly.
  2. Incorrect Details will led to cancellation of your request.
  3. After submitting all the required details, Our typical response time is 6-7 working days for Bonafide Applications, but it may be longer during peak academic periods.

Documents & Details Required

Please ensure you have the following information and documents ready prior to initiating the application:

  • 👤 Student Name (as per IIT Patna records)
  • 🆔 Roll Number
  • 🎓 Program & Semester
  • 📧 Valid IIT Patna Email ID
  • 📝 Reason for Bonafide Certificate
  • 🧾 Semester Fee Receipt

📌 Ensure all documents are correct and up-to-date before submission to avoid delays.

Enquiries & Verification Contact

For status tracking of submitted certificate applications, email the Academic Section at admissions_cet@iitp.ac.in or visit Room No. 301, 3rd Floor, Admin Block, IIT Patna Bihta Campus during official office hours (09:00 AM – 05:30 PM, Monday through Friday).
